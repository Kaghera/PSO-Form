Coverage for Loss or Damage

Packages shipped via UPS may be covered by our Pack and Ship Guarantee, or Shipment Protection for articles such as personal effects, breakables, antiques, and artwork. Articles that have been packed by Us are eligible for comprehensive coverage for both loss and damage. Our Pack and Ship Guarantee provides coverage up to a maximum of $10,000 for the value of the contents plus the cost of packaging and shipping. Articles that have been packed by Us may be covered for their full replacement value. Shipment Protection coverage is available for pre-packed articles which have not been packed by Us are eligible for limited coverage protecting for loss only. Coverage for pre-packed articles is limited to the actual cash value with proper deduction for depreciation up to a maximum of $2,500. Coverage for UPS shipments are sold in whole units of $100 each based on the value declared. All coverage is based on Canadian currency. When shipping via an alternative Carrier We will obtain Declared Value coverage for your shipment through the Carrier designated on this Parcel Shipping Order or from an independent company. Please keep in mind that We apply a surcharge to the cost of this product. The Declared Value terms and conditions for the various carriers and any applicable independent company can be found in the Carrier Service Guide (for coverage provided by the Carrier) and are also available for review at this The UPS Store® centre. Upon request, You may review such terms and conditions. Declared Value coverage is sold in whole units of $100 each. All coverage is based on Canadian currency.
